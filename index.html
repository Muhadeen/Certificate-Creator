Certificate Studio

Design certificates and speaker citations in your organization's style, then download or email them in one click.

1. Your organization

These details are printed on your documents and saved in this browser. In the wording you can use {name}, {event}, {org}, {date} and {topic}.

2. Signatories

Add everyone who signs. They are spaced evenly along the bottom. For each person, you can choose a signature image.

3. Design and live preview

Pick a style, then adjust colors, fonts and sizes. The preview updates as you change things.

4. Participants and certificates

Upload an Excel or CSV file with columns named Name and Email.

Email connection (one-time setup)

Paste the Web app URL from your Google Apps Script and your secret passcode.

5. Speaker citations

Add each speaker with their photo. Email is optional; without it you can still download the citation.

6. Bulk email

Send updates about the conference to everyone, without a certificate. Use {name} to greet each person. It sends to your participant list from section 4, unless you choose a different file here.
